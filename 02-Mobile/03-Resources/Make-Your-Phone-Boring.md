# Make Your Phone Boring

Are you tired of being distracted by your phone? Here are some tips to help you reduce this.

#### 1. Disable app notifications that you don't want to see.

You can fine-tune what apps notify you for inside the app or by holding the app icon and clicking "app info".

#### 2. Make your phone less colorful.

Consider making your background pure black, using a minimal icon pack (such as [Arcticons](https://arcticons.com/)), or enabling a black-and-white filter (Settings > Accessibility > Color and motion > Color correction > Grayscale).

#### 3. Use privacy-respecting FOSS apps.

Big tech makes money off of you being distracted, so consider finding [alternative apps](https://alternativeto.net/) built to benefit you instead.

#### 4. Make it easier to find your apps.

You can search for apps by swiping up on the home screen. You can organize your apps into folders by holding down an app icon and moving it over another, so you don't have to swipe through pages of random apps. Consider uninstalling or hiding apps you don't use often.

Alternatively, use a minimal launcher such as [Niagara Launcher](https://niagaralauncher.app/). Instead of memorizing where your apps or folders are, you can swipe to the letter of the alphabet your app name starts with. This reduces the likelihood that your eyes wander over a different app that grabs your attention, allowing you to be more focused.
