## What is this?

- A repository of the technology needed to be a sovereign individual.

- All methods here are battle tested and practiced in the daily lives of sovereign individuals.

- We vow to never advise usage of experimental or untested methods.

- We vow to meet users at their current skill level, and educate users on powerful tools as they progress.

- We stand to protect privacy, liberty, and financial freedom.

## How can I use The Sovereign Repository?

- For every day tasks that take time looking up.

- For friends and family who are wanting to learn more.

- For presentations and topics of discussion woth your local community.

- For creating educational material to share.

## What difficulty level should I start with?

- If you are new to these concepts please start with the Beginner level guides and work your way up.

- If you are familiar with these concepts then skip to higher difficulty guides.

- Read through all Resources and watch videos for additional information.
